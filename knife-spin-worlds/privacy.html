Privacy Policy for Knife Spin Worlds

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Knife Spin Worlds ("the app") handles information. The app is published by NordicPro Apps, a brand of NordicPro Active, Sweden ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address or any other personal information, and you do not need an account to play. Your game progress (levels, stars, coins and knives) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. Google uses this information as described in its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Apart from the advertising described above, the app does not send your data to anyone.

Deleting your data

All game data is stored on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at nordicproapps@hotmail.com.
